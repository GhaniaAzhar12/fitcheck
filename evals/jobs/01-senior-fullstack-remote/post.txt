Senior Full-Stack Engineer (TypeScript) – Remote
Company: Ledgerline (fictional sample post)

About us
Ledgerline builds invoicing and cash-flow software for 4,000+ small businesses across Europe. We're a 40-person, fully remote team and we ship every day.

The role
You'll own features end to end: from the Postgres schema to the React UI. You'll work closely with design and product, and mentor two mid-level engineers.

What you'll do
- Build and ship product features in our Next.js app and Node.js API
- Design database schemas and write efficient SQL
- Improve performance and reliability of our payments flows
- Review code and help shape our engineering practices

What we're looking for
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong TypeScript, React and Next.js
- Experience building REST APIs with Node.js
- Solid PostgreSQL knowledge (schema design, indexing, query tuning)
- Experience with Stripe or another payments provider
- Comfortable writing tests (Jest, Playwright or similar)
- Clear written English; you'll work async across time zones

Nice to have
- Prisma or Drizzle ORM
- Experience with AWS (Lambda, RDS, S3)
- You've worked on a B2B SaaS product before
- Experience integrating LLM APIs into product features

Where
Fully remote. You must overlap at least 4 hours with CET (UTC+1). We hire as contractors outside the EU.

Salary
€70,000 – €90,000 per year, depending on experience. Plus 30 days paid leave and a €1,500 learning budget.

How to apply
Send your CV and a short note about something you've built that you're proud of.
